## 4. Key Preliminary Findings

The simulated review identifies several areas requiring additional information or corrective action:

1. **Access management:** Periodic user and privileged-access reviews are not consistently demonstrated.
2. **Data protection:** Encryption implementation, data classification, and secure deletion practices require stronger evidence.
3. **Vulnerability and patch management:** Scanning coverage, patch timeliness, and remediation practices need validation.
4. **Security monitoring:** Log retention and alert investigation procedures are not fully demonstrated.
5. **Incident response and recovery:** Incident response exercises, backup restoration, and disaster recovery testing require supporting records.
6. **Third-party oversight:** The complete subcontractor inventory and independent security assurance remain unverified.

## 5. Evidence Limitations

This exercise uses simulated evidence descriptions rather than actual documents. The assessment therefore demonstrates how a GRC analyst could record vendor statements, evaluate evidence sufficiency, and identify follow-up requirements.

A real assessment would request appropriate supporting documents, review them for scope, currency, completeness, and reliability, and document any limitations. The Not Verified status does not, by itself, prove that a control is absent or ineffective.
