# Vendor Profile and Business Criticality Assessment

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the business profile, proposed service relationship, data sensitivity, and preliminary criticality of MedCloud Solutions. The information will be used to determine the scope and depth of the third-party cybersecurity risk assessment.

## 2. Vendor Information

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Organization | Northstar Health Network |
| Vendor Name | MedCloud Solutions |
| Vendor Type | Cloud Software / SaaS Provider |
| Industry | Healthcare Technology |
| Service Provided | Cloud-based patient record management software |
| Relationship Status | Proposed; not yet approved |
| Assessment Type | Initial Third-Party Cybersecurity Assessment |
| Assessment Status | In Progress |
| Information Source | Simulated vendor profile and assessment assumptions |

## 3. Business Relationship

Northstar Health Network is considering MedCloud Solutions as a provider of cloud-based patient record management software. The proposed service is intended to support healthcare staff in accessing and managing patient information.

Because the service will process sensitive information and support important healthcare operations, Northstar must evaluate the vendor's security controls before approving the relationship.

## 4. Information and Systems Involved

The proposed relationship may involve:

- Patient records and associated sensitive information.
- User accounts and authentication information.
- Healthcare staff access to the cloud service.
- Data exchanges between the vendor service and Northstar systems.
- Vendor-hosted infrastructure supporting the service.

The precise data fields, integration architecture, data locations, and vendor access arrangements would require confirmation during a real assessment. For this simulated project, these details will be treated as assessment assumptions until explicitly established.

## 5. Business Criticality Assessment

| Assessment Factor | Preliminary Rating | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Data Sensitivity | High | The service is expected to process sensitive patient information. |
| Business Criticality | High | The service supports patient record management and healthcare operations. |
| System Connectivity | Moderate to High | Integration with internal healthcare systems is anticipated but requires confirmation. |
| Regulatory Exposure | High | Inadequate protection of patient information could create privacy and compliance consequences. |

## 6. Preliminary Vendor Risk Tier

**Assigned Tier: High**

The proposed vendor is provisionally classified as high risk because of the sensitivity of the information involved, the importance of the supported business process, and the potential consequences of a security incident or service disruption.

This is an initial inherent-risk classification based on the proposed relationship. It is not a final security assessment result and does not establish whether the vendor's controls are effective.

## 7. Assessment Implications

The preliminary high-risk classification indicates that the assessment should include:

1. A detailed cybersecurity questionnaire.
2. Review of relevant security policies and supporting evidence.
3. Evaluation of identity and access management controls.
4. Assessment of data protection and encryption.
5. Review of vulnerability management and security monitoring.
6. Evaluation of incident response and business continuity.
7. Assessment of privacy practices and subcontractor risk management.
8. Documented risk ratings, remediation actions, and an approval recommendation.

## 8. Assumptions and Limitations

MedCloud Solutions and Northstar Health Network are fictional organizations created for portfolio demonstration.

The profile contains simulated business assumptions, not independently verified vendor information. In an actual assessment, the GRC analyst would validate the service description, data flows, integrations, hosting arrangements, contractual obligations, and business criticality with relevant stakeholders.

The preliminary risk tier may be revised if validated information materially changes the assessment.
